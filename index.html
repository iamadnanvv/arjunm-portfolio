01 — ABOUT

Every frame is a decision.
I try to make the right ones.

I'm Arjun M, a cinematographer and visual editor working out of Kozhikode, Kerala. My work sits at the intersection of light, movement and rhythm — from handheld documentary energy to controlled, deliberate frames built for brand films and short fiction. I care less about the gear and more about what a shot makes someone feel, and I carry that same instinct into the edit bay, where the real story gets found.
